Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026 · Applies to the Chrona Android app and this website

Short version: Chrona calculates your age entirely on your device. Nothing you type, including your date of birth, saved history, or settings, is ever sent to us or stored on any server. The only data collection comes from Google's advertising SDK, described below.

Data you enter into the app

Dates of birth, calculation history, favorites, and app settings (theme, accent color, etc.) are stored only in your device's local app storage. This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us, and we have no way to see, collect, or recover it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it permanently.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Chrona shows ads served by Google AdMob to support free use of the app. To do this, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App usage data related to ad requests and interactions (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required (for example, users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the app shows a consent form before any ad-related data is collected, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options inside the app. You can also opt out of personalized advertising, or reset your advertising identifier, from your device's system settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android).

Remote ad configuration

On each launch, the app fetches a small configuration file from our GitHub Pages hosting to determine which ad units are active. This request does not include any personal data beyond what any standard web request contains (such as your IP address, logged by GitHub per GitHub's Privacy Statement).

What we don't do

  • We don't require or collect an account, name, or email address.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking SDK.
  • We don't sell any data: we don't have any of yours to sell.

Children's privacy

Chrona is not directed at children and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us in error, contact us using the details below and we will address it, though as described above, we do not operate any server-side storage of user data in the first place.

Your choices

  • Ad personalization: Settings → Ad privacy options (in-app) or your device's ad settings.
  • All app data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage from your device's App info screen.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
